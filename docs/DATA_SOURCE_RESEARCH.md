## 2. What "OTC" actually is on Pocket Option

OTC assets (e.g. "EUR/USD OTC") are **synthetic, broker-generated instruments** quoted
24/7 including weekends. Key verified facts:

- Quotes are "generated by Pocket Option servers based on historical market data and
  mathematical algorithms" and are "not linked to the real exchange market in real
  time," so external news does not move them.
  Sources: [pocketoption.cx/trading](https://pocketoption.cx/trading/) (mirror),
  search-indexed [PO blog "What is OTC"](https://pocketoption.com/blog/en/interesting/trading-strategies/what-is-otc-in-pocket-option/),
  [EarnForex forum](https://www.earnforex.com/forum/threads/iq-otc-trading-bot-your-weekend-playbook.54849/).
- PO's **own Public Offer Agreement** states the Company "carries quoting of the Clients
  by using its own paid sources of quotations, applying processing of the quote flow"
  and that "quotes of any other companies, and/or quotes taken from other paid sources,
  cannot be taken into account when considering disputes."
  Source: [pocketoption.com/en/public-offer](https://pocketoption.com/en/public-offer/) (fetched).
- Each broker generates its *own* feed, so "EUR/USD OTC" on Pocket Option is a different
  time series than on Quotex or IQ Option. There is no canonical external reference.

**Implication:** Even a perfectly-licensed real EUR/USD feed (OANDA, Dukascopy, etc.)
is **not** the series PO settles against. Training on real FX and predicting PO's
synthetic OTC feed is exactly the misrepresentation project rule 1.6 forbids.

### 2.1 The economics (why a naive win-rate target is misleading)

- Win pays a fixed % of stake (Pocket Option markets "up to 92%"; real per-asset
  payouts observed 42%–96%, many well below 80%). A loss forfeits **100%** of stake.
- Breakeven win rate = `1 / (1 + payout)`:
  - 92% payout → **52.08%**
  - 80% payout → **55.56%**
  - 61% payout → **62.11%**
  - 42% payout → **70.42%**
- The broker is the **direct counterparty** to every trade. Regulators cite an
  "embedded conflict of interest" and documented "manipulation of price data to cause
  customers to lose."
  Sources: [CFTC/SEC alert](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/fraudadv_binaryoptions.html),
  [ESMA prohibition](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-agrees-prohibit-binary-options-and-restrict-cfds-protect-retail-investors).
- ESMA/ASIC found **74–89% of retail accounts lose money**.

A model would need to clear the breakeven hurdle *and* survive a counterparty that can
change payouts intraday and (per regulators) has manipulated settlement. This is a far
harder bar than "beat 50%."

## 3. Official API availability — **none**

- PO's homepage (one successful fetch) has "no API or developer documentation… no
  dedicated developer section, API documentation portal, or automation/integration
  tools." A `site:pocketoption.com API` search surfaces zero official developer pages.
  Source: [pocketoption.com/en](https://pocketoption.com/en/).
- PO's own blog is indexed stating it **"doesn't offer direct API access"** while
  enabling OTC trading only through its UI. (Search-snippet; page fetch blocked.)
  Source: [PO blog "Forex Trading APIs for 2025"](https://pocketoption.com/blog/en/knowledge-base/learning/forex-trading-apis-for-2025-best-broker-trade-options/).
- The only in-platform export is the user's **own trade history** (CSV/XLS) — account
  records, **not** market/quote data.
  Source: [traderinsight.pro](https://traderinsight.pro/blog/import-pocket-option).
- Official "automation" is limited to an in-app "AI Trading" toggle and a Telegram
  signal bot — neither is a data or trading API.

**Adversarial verdict — "no official API":** refuted = **false** (claim stands),
confidence high. A dedicated refutation agent searched for a developer portal, launch
announcement, or Swagger docs and found none; the only contrary source was a
content-farm article with no citation.

## 4. The unofficial ecosystem (documented as a *non-path*, not a how-to)

Surveyed for the report only; **AI AURA will not use any of these.** Descriptive facts:

- Every "Pocket Option API" on GitHub/PyPI is **unofficial and reverse-engineered**
  (ChipaDevTeam/PocketOptionAPI, Mastaaa1987/PocketOptionAPI-v2, lu-yi-hsun,
  A11ksa/API-Pocket-Option, taisprestes01, pypi `pocket-option`, `pocketoptionapi-async`).
- They authenticate by having the user open a logged-in browser, open DevTools, and copy
  the raw Socket.IO auth frame (`42["auth",{"session":"…","uid":…}]`, the "SSID") — or by
  driving Selenium/pywebview to log in and **solve the reCAPTCHA** automatically. There
  are no issued API keys because "PocketOption only supports SSID login, because Google
  reCAPTCHA." Automating past a CAPTCHA/anti-bot gate is explicitly disallowed by project
  rule 1.5 and the assistant's operating constraints.
- **Fragility is documented:** the two most-starred repos were **archived April 2026**;
  `BinaryOptionsTools-v2` issue **#122** shows the server returning
  `42["NotAuthorized"]` across multiple accounts/IPs starting **July 2026** — a
  platform-side change that broke the clients.
  Source: [issue #122](https://github.com/ChipaDevTeam/BinaryOptionsTools-v2/issues/122).
- Third-party guides state PO "does not officially support third-party automation" and
  that unauthorized bots can lead to "account suspension or permanent bans."

**Why this is not a legitimate path:** ToS-sanctioned (§4 below), requires defeating a
bot-detection/CAPTCHA gate, ties the platform to a single scraped session that breaks
without notice, and depends on an unregulated counterparty. It fails project rules 1.2,
1.5, 1.6 and the assistant's security constraints simultaneously.

## 5. Terms of Service

The operative document is the **"Service Terms & Public Offer"**
([pocketoption.com/en/public-offer](https://pocketoption.com/en/public-offer/), fetched).

- **Automation clause (verbatim, verified across 3 extraction passes):** the Company may
  cancel/revise results where *"The Trading Operation is performed with the help of
  **unauthorized bot software**."* The agreement does **not** define what makes bot
  software "authorized."
- **Anti-fraud clause:** prohibits activity "aimed at gaining profit using operations not
  instructed by the Company, **vulnerabilities in the Company's official website(s)**,
  bonus speculation, and abusive trading."
- The current agreement contains **no explicit** reverse-engineering/scraping clause
  (keyword sweep found none); the stronger "spider/AI analysis" prohibition circulates
  only in older/secondhand copies and could not be verified in the current text.
- Governing jurisdiction stated as **Costa Rica**.

**Adversarial verdict — "ToS prohibits unauthorized automation":** refuted = **false**
(claim stands), confidence high. The bots prohibition is directly citable; the
scraping-specific prohibition is plausible but not verbatim-confirmed in the *current*
document. Either way, nothing supports a *permission* to automate.

> ⚠️ These quotes were extracted through WebFetch's summarization layer (raw HTML egress
> was blocked). Wording was stable across passes, but before relying on ToS language for
> any decision, re-read the agreement directly from a permitted region.

## 6. Regulatory status (why the counterparty matters)

Pocket Option (Gembell Limited → PO Trade Ltd / PO TRADE LLC → Infinite Trade LLC;
Marshall Islands / Saint Lucia / Comoros / Costa Rica) holds **no license from any major
regulator**. Its cited credentials are an **IFMRRC "certificate"** (a private, non-
governmental body) and a **Mwali/MISA offshore license** — neither recognized by top-tier
regulators. Documented actions **against** it:

| Regulator | Action | Source |
|---|---|---|
| US CFTC | RED List, 2022-07-06 (soliciting US customers unregistered) | [cftc.gov/REDlist](https://www.cftc.gov/REDlist/node/241216) |
| UK FCA | Unauthorized-firm warning (2021-09-29, upd. 2026-02-10) | [fca.org.uk/warnings/pocketoption](https://www.fca.org.uk/news/warnings/pocketoption) |

| Spain CNMV | Unregistered-entity warning, 2024-08-28 | [cnmv.es PDF](https://www.cnmv.es/webservices/verdocumento/ver?t=%7B706b13d2-c72c-4af1-bdc8-ccaced94946b%7D) |

EEA, USA, Israel, UK, Philippines, Japan, or Brazil.
